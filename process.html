This isn't theory. It's the exact playbook we follow with every client, every time.

1
Week 1-2

Foundation

Goal: Shop optimised, tracking live, baseline set

Before we scale, we build. We audit your entire shop - listings, images, pricing, offers, fulfilment. We fix what's broken, optimise what's working, and set up tracking so we know exactly what's driving sales.

We analyse your competitors, identify winning content formats in your category, and build a hook library of proven angles that convert.

What You Get:

  • Fully optimised shop (listings, images, offers)
  • Tracking & analytics setup (pixel, UTMs, weekly reports)
  • Competitor & content audit
  • Hook library & content brief
  • Baseline metrics documented
2
Week 2-4

Affiliate Recruitment

Goal: 20-30 creators onboarded, first content live

We recruit, vet, and onboard 20-30 high-quality TikTok creators in your niche. We send them product, brief them on what works, and manage the entire process from outreach to posting.

No vanity metrics. We target creators with real engagement, authentic audiences, and a track record of driving sales.

What You Get:

  • 20-30 vetted creators onboarded
  • Product sent & creator onboarding complete
  • 20+ pieces of content live
  • First sales tracked by creator
  • WhatsApp/Telegram group for fast communication
3
Week 4-8

Organic Scale

Goal: Consistent content, affiliate momentum, GMV growing

Now we scale. 3-5 new pieces of creator content every week, weekly performance reviews, and constant optimisation. We double down on what's working - top creators get more product, higher commission, exclusive offers.

We run monthly affiliate competitions, share trending sounds and formats, and gamify performance to keep momentum high.

If budget exists, we start testing Spark Ads - boosting the best organic content with small daily budgets to accelerate growth.

What You Get:

  • 50+ total pieces of content live
  • Predictable weekly GMV
  • Top 5 performing creators identified
  • Weekly content & performance reports
  • Spark Ads testing (if budget exists)
4
Week 8-12

Paid Scale

Goal: Profitable ads running, combined GMV £30k+/month

Organic momentum is built. Now we pour fuel on the fire. We launch TikTok ad campaigns using the best-performing organic content as creative. UGC-style, authentic, proven to convert.

We start small (£20-50/day), test 5-10 videos, scale what works. If a campaign hits 3x ROAS for 3 days, we increase budget 20%. If it drops below 2x, we pause and refresh creative.

The goal: profitable paid ads that amplify organic success, not replace it.

What You Get:

  • Profitable TikTok ad campaigns live
  • £500-2000/week ad spend (depending on margin)
  • ROAS >2.5x minimum
  • Combined organic + paid GMV £30k+/month
  • Weekly ad performance reports
